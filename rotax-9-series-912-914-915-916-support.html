ROTAX 9 Series Support Guide — 912 / 914 / 915 / 916

Use this page as the ROTAX 9 Series model-family guide. It separates the 912 / 914 carbureted path from 912 iS / 915 iS / 916 iS electronically managed engines, then routes owners to the correct systems-support or service page.

Model-family Routing

Start with the Engine Family, Then the Symptom.

The 9 Series label covers different installations. Carbureted 912 / 914 aircraft, injected 912 iS aircraft, and turbocharged 914 / 915 / 916 aircraft do not all start from the same troubleshooting path.
