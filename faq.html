What does Lima Charlie Aero actually support?

Lima Charlie Aero supports light-sport aircraft owners and buyers with ROTAX 9-Series engine-system support, aircraft records review, pre-buy evaluation, E-PROPS support, avionics planning, and S-LSA / E-LSA / experimental support planning.

  • The practical focus is documentation, configuration, symptoms, and the next safe maintenance step.
  • Final scope depends on the aircraft, records, manuals, operating limitations, and applicable certificate privileges.

Is Lima Charlie Aero only a ROTAX 912 shop?

No. The site is ROTAX-specialized, but the support scope is broader than one engine model.

  • Current ROTAX focus includes the 912, 914, 915 iS, and 916 iS families.
  • Light-sport aircraft support may also involve records, avionics, propeller coordination, inspection planning, and owner support.

Where are you based?

Lima Charlie Aero LLC is based at KAKH / Gastonia Municipal Airport in Gastonia, North Carolina.

  • Service is by appointment.
  • For aircraft away from KAKH, start with the aircraft location, airport, issue, and timeline.

Do you provide mobile or field support?

Mobile or owner-coordinated support may be considered by appointment.

  • Whether it makes sense depends on the aircraft location, scope, tools, parts, hangar access, airport rules, weather, and documentation.
  • Some issues are better handled by records review first before travel or parts decisions are made.

What ROTAX engines do you support?

Lima Charlie Aero supports owner intake and maintenance planning for ROTAX 9-Series aircraft engines.

  • Common intake includes 912, 914, 915 iS, and 916 iS systems.
  • Final work scope depends on the installed aircraft, applicable ROTAX instructions, records, and certificate privileges.

What common ROTAX problems can you help evaluate?

Common ROTAX support starts with the symptom and the aircraft records, not with a generic parts guess.

  • Typical intake includes mag-drop or ignition symptoms, rough running, fuel-pressure concerns, carb balance, charging faults, ECU or EMS warnings, cooling or oil-temperature concerns, leaks, rubber/hose age, gearbox questions, and propeller vibration.
