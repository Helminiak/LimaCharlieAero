01 · Method

One aircraft, many systems. We trace how they interact.

A rough-running engine can be fuel, ignition, cooling, a propeller or a wiring fault, and the right fix depends on the aircraft's category, records and approvals. We follow the problem across those boundaries before choosing a part or a repair.

  1. Establish the configurationExact aircraft, engine, gearbox, propeller and installed equipment, plus category and operating limitations.
  2. Gather evidence before conclusionsWhat changed, under what conditions, with measurements and photographs rather than guesses.
  3. Evaluate the interactionsFuel with cooling, ignition with power supply, propeller with gearbox, records with authorization.
  4. Act within the approved pathManufacturer instructions first; where the path is unclear, we take the question to the manufacturer's engineering staff.
  5. Verify and documentRe-measure, run, inspect, and leave a record the next mechanic can follow.
