The LCA Standard

We don't stop at the part that failed.

An aircraft is a system of systems. A fuel symptom can be a cooling problem; a vibration can be a propeller, a gearbox or a mount. We trace how the systems interact, check the records and the approval path, and work the problem until the next step is supportable.
