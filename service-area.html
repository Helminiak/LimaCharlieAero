Base and Range

KAKH is the Anchor Point.

Lima Charlie Aero LLC works from KAKH / Gastonia Municipal Airport, which keeps the home base close to Gastonia, Charlotte, and nearby North Carolina and South Carolina airports. That location is useful for light-sport owners who need document-driven support, ROTAX 9 Series review, appointment planning, or a first pass on whether the aircraft should come to KAKH or be reviewed at another field.

The practical service range is not defined by a map alone. Aircraft condition, urgency, available parts, weather, hangar access, airport rules, travel time, and the tools available at the field all affect whether mobile or owner-coordinated support makes sense.

Airfield Readiness

Travel Depends on the Work Environment.

Some support can begin remotely with records, symptoms, and aircraft details. Some work needs the airplane, appropriate tools, power, lighting, safe access, and enough time to keep the work controlled. If the aircraft is away from KAKH, the support request should include the airport, hangar situation if known, urgency, and a short description of the concern.

When the tools and airfield conditions are suitable, Lima Charlie Aero LLC can consider flying to the aircraft by appointment. When the scope points to deeper access, special tools, parts staging, or weather-sensitive work, bringing the aircraft to a better-supported location may be the cleaner path.
