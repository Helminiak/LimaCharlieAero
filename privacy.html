Privacy Policy

Lima Charlie Aero LLC starts with a short support request and asks for aircraft records or photos only when they are needed for maintenance, inspection, troubleshooting, prebuy, or records-review questions.

Information Collected

The support request form may collect name, best contact information, aircraft location, N-number, engine model, need type, and a short description of what is happening.

Why Records/Photos May Be Requested

Follow-up records and photos can help evaluate aircraft identity, operating limitations, inspection status, ROTAX maintenance history, service bulletin or safety directive questions, and feasibility of next steps.

How Contact Information is Used

Contact information is used to respond to the aircraft request, ask follow-up questions, coordinate records review, or discuss appointment feasibility. Customer data is not sold.

No Authorization by Submission

Submitting records, photos, or aircraft details does not authorize work, create a maintenance release, or confirm return-to-service authority.

Deletion Requests

Aircraft owners may request deletion of submitted contact details, records, or photos. Some records may be retained only as needed for business, legal, or maintenance-documentation purposes.

Form Handling

The support request form is processed by the website form provider. Notification routing and spam protection are managed through the hosting account.
