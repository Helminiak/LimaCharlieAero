First Review

What Happens After You Send It?

You'll get a follow-up by call, text, or email. I'll review the aircraft, airport, engine model, symptom, and planning context first. If records or photos are needed, I'll ask for them after that review.

What to Have Ready

  • Aircraft type / N-number
  • Airport or aircraft location
  • Engine model
  • What changed and when it happens
  • Recent maintenance, if known
